3.19.[Lenstra, Granville] Show that if an odd number n be divisible by the square of some prime, then W(n), the least witness for n, is less than ln(n)**2.
    (Hint: Use (1.45).)
    This exercise is re-visited in Exercise 4.28.
    必要条件牜含平方因子
    充分条件牜无平方因子
    xxx:判定法牜无平方因子

[n:<-[3,5..]]:
  [W(n) =[def]= min{w | [[w:<-[0..]][not is_SPRP_(w;n)]]}]
[n:<-[1..]]:
  [is_squarefree(n) =[def]= [@[d:<-[2..<n]] -> [n%d**2 =!= 0]]]
  #or:[is_squarefree(n) =[def]= [mu(n) =!= 0]]

[[[n:<-[3,5..]][?[d:<-[2..]] -> [n%d**2 == 0]]] -> [W(n) < ln(n)**2]]
[[[n:<-[3,5..]][W(n) >= ln(n)**2]] -> [@[d:<-[2..]] -> [n%d**2 =!= 0]]]
[[[n:<-[3,5..]][W(n) >= ln(n)**2]] -> [is_squarefree(n)]]
[[[n:<-[3,5..]][@[base:<-[2..<min(n,ceil(ln(n)**2))]] -> [is_SPRP_(base;n)]]] -> [is_squarefree(n)]]
