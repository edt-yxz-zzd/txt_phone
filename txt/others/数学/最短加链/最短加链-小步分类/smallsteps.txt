Trivially holds:  k > i  ==>  d_{i,k} = 0
d_{i,k}=0   ==>  \forall j >= k:  d_{i,j} = 0
\sum_{k=0}^i d_{i,k} = 2^i

By definition:
\sum_{k=0}^r d_{r-k,k} = d(r)
mean_i = 2^-i * \sum_{k=0}^i k*d_{i,k}

Conjectures:
d_{i,k}=0   ==>  \forall h <= i:  d_{h,k} = 0
d_{i+1,k} >= d_{i,k}
\forall k \exists C_k > 0:   \lim_{i -> \infty}  d_{i,k} / i^k = C_k
